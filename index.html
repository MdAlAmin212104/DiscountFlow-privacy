Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

Shopify App Store Privacy Compliance Guaranteed

This Privacy Policy describes how DiscountFlow – Smart Discount ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards merchant and store data when you install or use our Shopify Application.

1. Overview & Data Philosophy

DiscountFlow is designed specifically for automated sale campaign scheduling and price restoration. We adhere strictly to a privacy-first approach, collecting only the minimal operational data required to execute sale discounts and preserve price history for your store variants.

2. Data Collected from Shopify Merchants

When you install and authorize DiscountFlow via Shopify OAuth, we collect and store the following specific categories of data:

A. Shop Information

  • Shop Domain & Shop ID: Unique Shopify store identifier (e.g., your-store.myshopify.com).
  • Shop Owner Email: Used exclusively for critical app notifications, billing receipts, and support communications.
  • Primary Currency & Timezone: Required to schedule sales according to your local business hours and store currency formatting.

B. Product & Inventory Data

  • Product IDs & Variant IDs: Identifiers of products included in your scheduled discount campaigns.
  • Original Variant Prices: Exact numerical price and compare-at-price values prior to campaign execution (used for safe price snapshots).
  • Campaign Configurations: Start times, end times, discount percentages, fixed amounts, and rule filters.

3. End-Customer Data Collection Declaration

DiscountFlow does NOT collect, store, track, or process personal identifiable information (PII) of your store's end-customers.

Our app operates entirely on backend product variant prices and inventory metadata. Customer names, email addresses, shipping addresses, payment details, and checkout histories are never accessed or retained by DiscountFlow.

4. Data Retention & Shopify Mandatory Webhooks Compliance

We retain merchant shop data and price snapshot records only for as long as the application remains installed on your Shopify store.

DiscountFlow fully supports and strictly adheres to Shopify's Mandatory GDPR / CCPA Compliance Webhooks:

  • customers/data_request: Since DiscountFlow stores zero end-customer PII, any customer data request returns an automated zero-record verification payload.
  • customers/redact: Confirms that zero customer PII exists within our cloud database structure.
  • shop/redact: Mandatory Store Erasure. Upon receiving a shop/redact webhook from Shopify (or automatically 48 hours following app uninstallation), our system permanently purges all database records, campaign histories, price snapshots, access tokens, and session keys associated with your store.

5. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between Shopify, your store, and DiscountFlow is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Database snapshots and authentication credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption hosted within secure cloud infrastructure with strict access controls.
